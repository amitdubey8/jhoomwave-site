Refunds & Cancellations

You hear a free preview of the style before paying, so most orders are final. We still want you happy.

Made-for-you songs

  • Every order includes one free re-make (new version or lyric fix) within 7 days.
  • If we fail to deliver within 24 hours, or the file is broken, you get a full refund.
  • If the name or details are wrong because of a mistake on our side, we fix it free.

Ready songs and passes

Ready songs and Booth Night Passes are delivered as soon as payment is confirmed and are not refundable, except when the download or pass does not work and we can't fix it.

Business plans

Cancel any time before the next billing date; the current month is not refunded. Yearly plans can be refunded pro rata within the first 30 days.

How to ask

Message us (website chat or Instagram DM @jhoomwave) with your name and the phone/email you ordered with. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method, through our payment partner (Razorpay, once online checkout is live), within 5–7 working days.
