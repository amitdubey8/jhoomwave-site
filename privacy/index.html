Privacy Policy

We collect only what we need to make your song and run the service.

What we collect

  • Order details: names, occasion, story, language and style you enter.
  • Contact: the WhatsApp number or email you give us.
  • Enquiries: the name, WhatsApp number or email and message you send through our website forms or the live chat. They are stored securely in our team system (Supabase, Mumbai region) and only our team can see them. Our AI assistant may draft the reply; a person reviews it.
  • Payments: handled by our payment partner (Razorpay, once online checkout is live); we receive the payment status, not your card or UPI details.
  • Usage: anonymous statistics such as pages viewed and songs played.

How we use it

To write lyrics and make your song, deliver it, send receipts, give support and improve the service. Your story text and names are sent to our music and voice provider only to generate your order.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data. We don't use children's details for anything other than their order. Camera features in the Booth are off by default and never identify faces.

Keeping and deleting

We keep order files so you can download them again. Ask us and we'll delete your details and files, except what the law requires us to keep for invoices.

Contact

JHOOMWAVE, India. The quickest way to reach us is the chat on this website (tap Chat with us, or "💬 Chat with our team" in Jhoomi) or an Instagram DM to @jhoomwave. Website: jhoomwave.com.
